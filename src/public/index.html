How RelayPulse Works

Autonomous Speed-to-Lead & Outreach Orchestration

Contacting a high-intent prospect within 5 minutes yields 8x higher conversion. Here is the automated lifecycle:

⚡ Step 1

Capture & AI-Score Leads

Prospect attributes (seniority, company size, pain points) are evaluated by the AI engine to compute a 0–100 score and intent tier (HOT, WARM, COLD).

🚀 Step 2

Automated Multi-Step Sequences

Outreach copy is tailored to the prospect's profile. Time-delayed follow-ups advance automatically with built-in speed-to-lead SLAs.

💬 Step 3

Smart Inbound Reply Routing

Webhook sentiment analysis routes replies: meeting bookings pause sequences and alert sales reps; opt-outs trigger instant suppression.

🔒 Step 4

Append-Only Audit Trail & Idempotency

Duplicate webhooks never double-trigger actions thanks to Idempotency-Key caching, while all mutations are logged in an immutable event ledger.
